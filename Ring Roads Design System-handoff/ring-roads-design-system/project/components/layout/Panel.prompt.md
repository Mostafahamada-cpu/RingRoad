Sectioning card for Ring Roads screens — white surface, soft shadow, colored accent bar on the leading edge. Group form fields or results inside.

```jsx
<Panel title="مبالغ المعاملة · Transaction amounts" accent="accent">
  <Input label="Buyer amount" ... />
</Panel>
<Panel title="النتائج · Results" accent="success"> ... </Panel>
```

Accent roles: `accent` (blue), `brand`, `success`, `warning`. In RTL the bar moves to the right edge automatically when `dir="rtl"`.
